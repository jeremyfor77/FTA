Hundred-Man Warzone Opens on Control Zone Front, Noobs Die Bravely and Often

The clan's new flagship ground: a 100-player WARDOGS server running three-team Control Zone warfare across a rotating map pool — open to the public, and merciless to anyone who spends their vest money on a truck.

Field Report — FTAWD100CONTROLZONE — Filed 26 September 2026

The Dispatch

The clan's WARDOGS server is live and set as the flagship rotation for the foreseeable future. Three teams, a hundred players, one Control Zone worth bleeding for — first side to 100 points wins, and every single respawn hands out a fresh $10,000 to blow on gear, weapons, and questionable vehicle purchases.

Early returns confirm the noobs are exactly as advertised: full sprint into an open Control Zone with the armor budget still sitting untouched in the bank, a truck purchased and immediately folded into the nearest ravine, and at least one friendly-fire incident per session where a teammate got mistaken for a silhouette at 200 meters. It happens to everyone once. It happens to some people every match.

The hardcore side of the roster is its own breed entirely — loadout math done before the round even loads, every Control Zone rotation memorized, and a documented moral crisis each time they die with cash still unspent. Expect both types in the same squad by week two, arguing over comms about who flipped the truck this time.

Connection details and the current server manifest live in the Comms column. No password, no application — direct connect and go.
